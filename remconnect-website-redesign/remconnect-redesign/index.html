GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We’ve got answers.

A few useful details to help you take your next step with confidence.

Visit RemConnect ↗
Is RemConnect really free for agents?

RemConnect’s public website states that agents do not pay to apply, train, or get placed. Employers pay for the service. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent RemConnect who asks for a fee.

Do I need prior call-center experience?

No prior call-center experience is required for some entry-friendly paths. You’ll need conversational English, a willingness to learn, and the ability to complete the assessment and training process.

How and when do I get paid?

Pay depends on the placement and agreement. RemConnect describes monthly pay in USD or ETB and states that it does not take a cut of an agent’s wage. Confirm the terms for a specific role before accepting it.

What equipment do I need?

You’ll generally need a laptop or desktop, a working headset with microphone, a webcam, reliable internet, and a quiet workspace. A power backup can be helpful but is not listed as a requirement to apply.

How long does the process take?

The public website describes around six weeks on average from application through training to a first shift. Timing can vary based on the cohort and your progress.

What if I’m not placed right away?

RemConnect describes continued coaching and ongoing matching for eligible agents while suitable opportunities are being found. Ask the team about the current process and terms for your cohort.
