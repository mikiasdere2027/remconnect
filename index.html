FAQ

What you'll need
to get started.

Reliable internet, a quiet workspace, a laptop with a headset, and conversational English. Don't tick every box yet? Apply anyway.

Talk to our team
Do I need prior call-center experience?

No. Most of our agents started with zero BPO experience. What matters is conversational English and a willingness to learn — our curriculum takes you from the fundamentals to client-ready.

Is RemConnect really free for agents?

Yes, completely. You never pay to apply, to train, or to get placed. We're paid by the employers who hire you. Anyone asking you for a fee is not RemConnect.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop (Windows or Mac), a headset with a mic, a webcam, reliable internet (10 Mbps+ recommended), and a quiet space. A power backup helps given local outages, but isn't required to apply.

How and when do I get paid?

Once you're placed, you're paid on a fixed monthly cycle. You choose USD or ETB, and your full wage reaches you — we never take a cut of what you earn.

How long does the process take?

On average about six weeks — from application, through training and certification, to your first shift.

What if I'm not placed right away?

You stay on the bench with continued coaching while we keep matching you to new openings — at no cost — until you're live with a client.
